Chapter 9 practice test

45 marks: 12 multiple-choice and 6 short-answer questions on heat transfer, waves, sound, hearing and the electromagnetic spectrum. The questions are new, but the skills are the same as the real test.

Choose how to do it

Use a pen and your workbook for working, as in the real test. Do the practice questions page first if you are not ready for this.

Formulas and data (given in the real test)

v = f × λ (wave speed = frequency × wavelength)
distance = speed × time
1 ms = 0.001 s   1 kHz = 1000 Hz

Speed of sound in air = 343 m/s. Speed of sound in seawater = 1500 m/s.
