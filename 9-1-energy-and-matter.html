9.1 Energy and matter

Goal: describe how thermal energy is transferred, using the particle model.
Textbook: Good Science 9, pp. 198–199.
Success criteria
  • MustI can define conduction, convection and radiation.
  • ShouldI can give an example of each and explain the role of matter in each type of transfer.
  • CouldI can decide which type of transfer mainly moves heat in solids, liquids and gases.

Predict first

A metal spoon and a wooden spoon sit in the same cup of hot water. Which handle will feel hot first?

Watch heat travel along a solid

Each dot is a particle in a metal rod. Switch the heater on and watch the left end. Brighter red means more energy.

Key terms: tap to reveal

Conduction: energy passes between touching particles, mostly in solids.
Convection: warm fluid rises and cool fluid sinks, forming a current.
Radiation: energy travels as waves and needs no matter.
Thermal conductivity: how quickly a material lets heat pass through it.
✏️ In your workbookMake a three-column table: Conduction, Convection, Radiation. In each, write what is moving (energy only, fluid, or waves) and one everyday example.
🎨 Draw & labelSketch the pot picture above. Add three coloured arrows, one for each type of transfer.

Use it: conductors and insulators

Metals have high thermal conductivity. Materials with low conductivity are insulators. Tap an item, then tap the box it belongs in.

Science in society

Double-glazed windows, insulated drink bottles and space blankets all slow heat transfer.

🔍 Research taskPick one of those technologies. Find out which type of transfer (conduction, convection or radiation) it reduces and how. Write 2–3 sentences.
