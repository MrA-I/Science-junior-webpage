Parts of a wave

Crest, trough, amplitude and wavelength

The amplitude is the height from the rest line to a crest.
The wavelength is the distance from one crest to the next crest. Its symbol is λ. It is measured in metres (m).
  • A crest is the top of a wave.
  • A trough is the bottom of a wave.
  • A bigger amplitude means the wave carries more energy.

The wave equation

Speed, frequency and wavelength

v = f × λ

SymbolMeansUnit
vspeedm/s
ffrequencyHz
λwavelengthm

You can turn the equation around:

To find f:
f = vλ
To find λ:
λ = vf
Worked example
A wave has f = 20 Hz and λ = 3 m. Find v.
v = f × λ
v = 20 × 3
v = 60 m/s

Case study

Tsunami waves

A tsunami is a huge ocean wave. Watch it move from deep sea to the shore.

  • In deep sea, the wave is fast. It has a long wavelength and is not very tall.
  • Near the shore, the water is shallow. The wave slows down.
  • The frequency stays the same, so the wavelength gets shorter.
  • The energy is squeezed into a smaller space.
