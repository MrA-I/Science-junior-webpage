Way 1

Conduction

Conduction is heat moving through a solid, from particle to particle.
  • The heat makes the particles at the hot end shake faster.
  • They bump into the next particles and make them shake too.
  • The heat moves along the rod. The particles stay in place.

Look: red particles are shaking fast. Blue particles are shaking slowly.

Way 2

Convection

Convection is heat moving when a hot liquid or gas rises.
  • Hot water rises.
  • At the top it cools down, so it sinks.
  • This makes a loop. The loop is called a convection current.

Look: red particles go up on the left. Blue particles come down on the right.

Way 3

Radiation

Radiation is heat moving as invisible waves. It does not need any particles.
  • Hot things send out invisible heat waves.
  • The waves can travel through empty space.
  • You don't need to touch the hot thing to feel it.

Look: the waves cross the empty gap and the can gets warmer.
