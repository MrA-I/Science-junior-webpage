Key word

What is ultrasound?

Ultrasound is sound that is too high for humans to hear. Its frequency is above 20 000 Hz.
  • Ultrasound is still sound. It is a vibration that needs a medium.
  • A medical probe uses about 2 000 000 Hz to 10 000 000 Hz.

How a scan works

Echoes from inside the body

An echo is sound that bounces back (reflects) from a surface.

The animation is slowed down. A real echo takes less than one thousandth of a second. µs means a millionth of a second.

  • The sound reflects where two different tissues meet. This is a boundary.
  • Some sound bounces back. The rest goes deeper.
  • A deeper boundary gives an echo that takes longer to come back.

How a scan works

The probe, the gel and the screen

No gel. There is air between the probe and the skin.

  • The probe has a transmitter. It sends out pulses of ultrasound.
  • The probe has a receiver. It detects the echoes.
  • Air reflects most of the sound. The gel fills the air gap so the sound gets in.
  • A computer uses the echoes to build the picture.

Why ultrasound?

Safe for a baby

Ultrasound is a sound wave. At scan levels, ultrasound carries low energy. It does not harm cells.
X-rays are electromagnetic waves. They carry high energy. They can harm cells.

This is why doctors use ultrasound to look at an unborn baby.

Another use

Doppler ultrasound

A Doppler flow meter uses ultrasound to measure how fast blood flows, and which way.
  • The probe always sends the same frequency.
  • The sound reflects off moving red blood cells.
  • The echo changes frequency. This is the Doppler effect from Lesson 5.
  • The change shows the speed and direction of the blood.

Finding out or treating

Diagnostic or therapeutic?

Diagnostic means used to find out what is wrong.
Therapeutic means used to treat or heal.
  • Therapeutic ultrasound warms tissue deep in the body. This helps it heal faster.
  • A surgical aspirator vibrates very fast. A brain surgeon uses it to break up a tumour (a lump of cells that should not be there). The pieces are sucked away.
