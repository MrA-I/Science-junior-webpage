  • When the buzzer comes towards you, you hear a higher pitch.
  • When it goes away from you, you hear a lower pitch.
  • The buzzer itself never changes its note.

Sound waves

A moving source

The Doppler effect is a change in the frequency you hear. It happens when the source moves towards you or away from you.
  • In front: the waves are bunched up. Shorter wavelength, higher frequency, higher pitch.
  • Behind: the waves are spread out. Longer wavelength, lower frequency, lower pitch.
  • The waves still travel at the same speed in air.

The Doppler effect at work

Police radar

  • A radar gun sends out radio waves.
  • The waves bounce off the moving car and come back.
  • The frequency of the waves that come back has changed.
  • A bigger change means the car is going faster.

The Doppler effect works for all waves. Doctors use it with ultrasound to see blood flow. Ultrasound is sound too high for people to hear. Weather radar uses it to track rain. Ships use sonar, which sends sound through water.
