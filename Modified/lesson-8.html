The spectrum

Seven kinds of waves

The electromagnetic spectrum is the full range of electromagnetic waves, in order of wavelength.
  • Radio waves have the longest wavelength.
  • Gamma rays have the shortest wavelength.
  • Visible light is the only part our eyes can see.

The pattern

Wavelength, frequency and energy

  • A photon is a tiny packet of electromagnetic energy.
  • All electromagnetic waves travel at the same speed in a vacuum (empty space): about 300 000 km/s.
  • Radio waves are not sound waves. They are electromagnetic waves.

X-rays

Images of bones

  • Bones absorb more X-rays than soft tissue.
  • So bones show up clearly on the image.
  • X-ray exposure is kept short because X-rays can damage cells.

Energy and safety

Lower or higher energy?

Waves with higher energy can damage cells. These are ultraviolet, X-rays and gamma rays.

We use sunscreen to protect our skin from UV. Lead aprons protect people during X-rays.

UV beads change colour in sunlight but not under the classroom lights.
