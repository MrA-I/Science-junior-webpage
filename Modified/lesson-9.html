Reflection

Light bounces off a mirror

Reflection is when light bounces off a surface.
  • The normal is a line at right angles to the mirror.
  • We measure the angles from the normal.
  • The angle in is called the angle of incidence.
  • The angle out is the angle of reflection. The two angles are always the same.

Refraction

Light bends at a boundary

Refraction is when light bends as it goes from one material into another.
  • Light slows down when it goes into glass or water.
  • The change in speed makes it bend.
  • It bends at the boundary, where the two materials meet. Inside the glass it goes straight.
  • Going into glass, it bends towards the normal.

What light can do

Four things light can do

When light hits something, it can do four things.

Absorption: the material takes in the light energy. It often warms up.
Reflection: the light bounces off. A mirror gives a clear image.
Refraction: the light bends as it goes into a new material.
Scattering: tiny particles send the light off in many directions.
  • A black T-shirt absorbs most light, so it gets hot in the sun.
  • Air scatters blue light the most. This is why the sky looks blue.

Light at work

Lenses

A lens is a curved piece of glass or plastic. It bends light.
  • A convex lens is thick in the middle. It brings rays together.
  • A concave lens is thin in the middle. It spreads rays apart.
  • Glasses, cameras and magnifying glasses all use lenses.
