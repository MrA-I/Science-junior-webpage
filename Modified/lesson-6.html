Hearing range

What can people hear?

People can hear sounds from about 20 Hz to 20 000 Hz. This is the human hearing range.
  • Below 20 Hz is infrasound. It is too low for people to hear.
  • Above 20 000 Hz is ultrasound. It is too high for people to hear.
  • Dogs can hear higher sounds than people.

Parts of the ear

Three sections

The ear has three sections: the outer ear, the middle ear and the inner ear.
  • Outer ear: the pinna and the ear canal.
  • Middle ear: the eardrum and three tiny bones called the ossicles.
  • Inner ear: the cochlea. The auditory nerve goes from here to the brain.

How the ear works

Sound through the ear

  • The sound wave makes the eardrum vibrate.
  • The ossicles pass on the vibrations and make them stronger.
  • Fluid in the cochlea moves. Tiny hair cells change this into electrical signals.
  • The auditory nerve carries the signals to the brain.

The middle ear

The ossicles

The ossicles are three tiny bones: the hammer, the anvil and the stirrup. They make the vibrations stronger.
  • Stronger vibrations carry more energy.
  • The ossicles do not change the frequency.

Looking after your ears

Hearing loss

Very loud sounds can damage the hair cells in the cochlea. They do not grow back.
  • The louder the sound, the faster it can cause damage.
  • Earmuffs and earplugs help protect your ears.
