How sound travels

Vibrations make sound

Sound is energy carried by vibrating particles. A vibration is a fast back and forth movement.
  • The speaker vibrates back and forth.
  • It pushes air particles together. This is a compression.
  • Then the particles spread out. This is a rarefaction.
  • Each particle passes the energy on to the next one.

Sound lab

Volume and pitch

Volume is how loud a sound is. It depends on the amplitude. It is measured in decibels (dB).
Pitch is how high or low a sound is. It depends on the frequency.
  • Louder sound: taller waves.
  • Higher pitch: waves closer together.
  • This picture is silent. Your teacher may play real sounds.
