The front of the eye

The pupil and the iris

The iris is the coloured ring. It is a muscle. It makes the pupil bigger or smaller.
  • In bright light, the pupil gets smaller. Less light gets in. This protects the back of the eye.
  • In dim light, the pupil gets bigger. More light gets in, so you can see.

Inside the eye

Parts of the eye

The cornea is the clear front of the eye. It does most of the bending of light.
The lens changes shape. It fine-tunes the focus so the picture is sharp.
  • The cornea and the lens bend light by refraction.
  • The retina is at the back. It changes light into electrical signals.
  • The optic nerve carries the signals to the brain.

The path of light

From the tree to the brain

Light from the tree goes into the eye.

  • Light comes from the object into the eye. Nothing comes out of the eye.
  • The retina changes light energy into electrical signals.
  • The brain uses the signals to make the picture you see.

Focusing

Near and far

The lens changes shape to focus. Small muscles around it change its shape.
  • Far object: the lens is thin.
  • Near object: the lens is thick.
  • A thick lens bends light more.

Rods and cones

Rods work in dim light. Rods detect shades of grey.
Cones need bright light. Cones detect colour.

Rods and cones are cells in the retina. They detect light.
