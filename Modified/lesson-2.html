Key word

The medium

The medium is the stuff a wave travels through. It can be air, water or rock.

Wave type 1

Transverse waves

In a transverse wave, the particles move across the way the wave moves. Here they move up and down.
  • The red particle moves up and down.
  • The wave moves across the screen.
  • Example: shaking a rope or a slinky up and down.

Wave type 2

Longitudinal waves

In a longitudinal wave, the particles move along the way the wave moves. They go back and forth.
  • The red particle moves back and forth.
  • Squashed together = compression.
  • Spread out = rarefaction.
  • Example: pushing and pulling a slinky. Sound is a longitudinal wave.

Two kinds of waves

Does it need a medium?

Mechanical waves need a medium. They cannot travel through empty space.
Examples: sound, water waves.
Electromagnetic waves do not need a medium. They can travel through empty space.
Examples: light, radio waves.

Empty space with no particles is called a vacuum.
