The four features

Tap a label. Frequency is not drawn here: it is how many waves pass a point each second, measured in hertz (Hz).

The wave equation

v = f × λ

speed (m/s) = frequency (Hz) × wavelength (m)

If the speed stays the same, a higher frequency means a shorter wavelength.

A wave has f = 20 Hz and λ = 3 m. Find v.
v = f × λ = 20 × 3 = 60 m/s.
To find f: f = v ÷ λ. To find λ: λ = v ÷ f.
Frequency: the number of complete waves passing a point each second.
Wavelength: the distance between matching points on neighbouring waves.
Amplitude: the height of a wave from its rest position.
Hertz: the unit of frequency. 1 Hz is one wave per second.
