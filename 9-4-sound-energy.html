9.4 Sound energy

Goal: model how sound waves travel and describe their properties.

Success criteria

How sound travels

Sound is energy that travels as a longitudinal wave through a medium. Tap each step.

Vibration→Particles pushed together (compression)→Particles spread out (rarefaction)→Energy passes on

The pattern of compressions and rarefactions can be drawn as peaks and troughs. Tap a graph.

Tap a wave to see what its shape tells you.
Sound: energy transferred by vibrating particles as a longitudinal wave.
Volume: how loud a sound seems. It depends on amplitude and is measured in decibels (dB).
Pitch: how high or low a sound seems. It depends on frequency (Hz).
