    Go further: waves, risks and technology

    Tap each card to read more.

    X-rays and gamma rays. They are not the same thing, and they are not equally dangerous. Both are high energy, but gamma rays usually have shorter wavelengths and more energy. Both are used in medicine under careful control.
    Staying safe. The more energy a wave carries, the more care is needed. Sunscreen and UV-blocking clothing protect from UV. Lead aprons and short exposure times protect people during X-rays.
    Looking at the universe. Telescopes detect radio waves, infrared, visible light, UV, X-rays and gamma rays. Each type shows different features of stars and galaxies.
