9.5 The Doppler effect

Goal: explain the Doppler effect and give examples of its applications to technology.
Textbook: Good Science 9, pp. 208–209.
Success criteria

  • ShouldI can explain the Doppler effect and its applications in technology.

Why the pitch changes

Go further: the Doppler effect at work

Tap each card to read more.

Police radar. A radar gun sends out radio waves. They bounce off a moving car and return with a slightly different frequency. The bigger the change, the faster the car.
Weather radar. Radio waves bounce off raindrops. The frequency change shows whether the rain is moving towards or away from the radar and how fast, which helps forecasters track storms.
Blood flow. Doctors use ultrasound that reflects off moving red blood cells. The change in frequency shows the speed and direction of blood flow.
Astronomy. Light from distant galaxies is shifted to longer wavelengths, called redshift. This suggests those galaxies are moving away from us, which is one piece of evidence that the universe is expanding.

Common mix-ups

  • The source does not change its own pitch. The observer detects a different frequency.
  • The wave speed in air does not change. The spacing between waves does.
  • Pitch is about frequency, not volume.

Doppler effect presentation
