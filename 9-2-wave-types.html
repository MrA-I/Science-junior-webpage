9.2 Wave types

Goal: explain what a wave is, and the difference between types of waves and how they transfer energy.
Textbook: Good Science 9, pp. 200–201.
Success criteria
  • MustI can define medium in the context of wave energy transfer.
  • ShouldI can describe how a wave transfers energy using the particle model.
  • CouldI can describe and compare transverse and longitudinal waves.

Predict first

A cork floats on the sea. A wave rolls past it towards the beach. What does the cork do?

✏️ In your workbookWrite your prediction, then draw the cork and the wave with an arrow for the wave’s direction.

Watch one particle

These dots are particles in a material. Follow the red particle. The ring shows where it started.

✏️ In your workbookComplete for each mode: “The red particle moves ________ (up and down / back and forth) while the wave travels ________.” Then answer: does the particle travel along with the wave?

Waves transfer energy, not matter

A wave carries energy from place to place. The particles of the medium only move a small distance and return. Tap parts of the diagrams.

Tap a label to see what it means.

Key terms: tap to reveal

Medium: the matter a wave travels through, such as air, water or rock.
Mechanical wave: a wave that needs a medium, for example sound or water waves.
Electromagnetic wave: a wave that can travel through a vacuum, for example light.
Vacuum: a space with no matter, no particles.
🎨 Draw & labelSketch a transverse and a longitudinal wave. Label the wave direction and the particle movement on each. On the longitudinal wave, label a compression and a rarefaction.
✏️ In your workbookCopy this definition: “A wave transfers ________ from one place to another without the net transfer of ________.”
