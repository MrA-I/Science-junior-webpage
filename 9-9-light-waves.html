Go further: light at work

Tap each card to read more.

Glasses and cameras. Lenses use refraction to bend light so it forms a sharp image, on the retina in the eye or on the sensor in a camera. The curve of the lens decides where the rays meet.
Optical fibres. Thin glass strands carry light signals long distances. The light keeps reflecting off the inside walls, so it follows the fibre instead of escaping.
The colour of the sky. Air scatters short-wavelength blue light much more than red light, so the daytime sky looks blue. At sunset the light passes through more air and the blue is scattered away, so more red reaches you.
