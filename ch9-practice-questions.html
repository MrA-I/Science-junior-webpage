Chapter 9 practice questions

Questions on every Chapter 9 idea, in the same style as the topic test, with new contexts. Each answer gives you feedback that explains why.

How to learn the most from these

  1. Try first. For calculations, write your working in your workbook before you type an answer.
  2. Rate how sure you are. Being sure and wrong is the best chance to fix a misconception.
  3. Read the feedback, especially when you are right. Check you can say why.
  4. Come back tomorrow and press “Review my misses”. Remembering a day later is what makes it stick.
Formulas and data (these are given in the test)

v = f × λ (wave speed = frequency × wavelength)
distance = speed × time
1 ms = 0.001 s   1 kHz = 1000 Hz

Speed of sound in air = 343 m/s. Speed of sound in seawater = 1500 m/s. Speed of light in a vacuum = 300 000 km/s.
